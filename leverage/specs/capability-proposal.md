## summary: The proposal
verdict: Fund the four capability layers as one programme, or accept that each market builds its own and none of them integrate.
- Four markets are building overlapping ingest and modelling layers today
- The duplication is PLACEHOLDER effort per market per year
- A shared model needs PLACEHOLDER funding and one accountable owner
- Ask: endorse the shared model, and name the owner

## divider: The current state

## capabilities: What exists today
### Ingest
- Market one pipeline
- Market two pipeline
- Market three pipeline
- Market four pipeline
### Model
- Four sets of metric definitions
### Consume
- Local dashboards
- Local exports
### Govern
- No shared lineage

## bullets: Why this does not scale
- Each market defines the same metrics differently, so nothing aggregates
- A change to a source system is four changes, made four times
- Governance is asserted locally and cannot be evidenced centrally

## divider: The proposal

## capabilities: Target capability model
### Ingest
- Shared contracts
- Quality gates
- One landing zone
### Model
- Semantic layer
- Central metric registry
### Consume

- Embedded views
- Market extensions
### Govern
- Access model
- Lineage
- Retention

- Business owner named
- Contracts mapped
- Metrics reconciled
- Cut over
- Local layer retired
